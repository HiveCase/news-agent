Global technology firm NovaTech reported record quarterly earnings on Tuesday, driven by strong growth in its cloud computing division. Revenue surged 28% year over year, beating analyst expectations and boosting the company's stock in early trading. Chief Executive Maria Alvarez praised the team's performance and said the company would expand hiring across its engineering units. Analysts described the results as a breakthrough quarter and expressed optimism about continued momentum. The company also announced a new partnership aimed at improving data-center efficiency.
