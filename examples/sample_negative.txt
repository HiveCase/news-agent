Shares of retailer BrightMart crashed nearly 20% on Wednesday after the company warned of a sharp decline in sales and announced sweeping layoffs. The struggling chain said weak consumer demand and rising costs had pushed it into a deepening crisis. Executives cut the annual profit forecast and cautioned that more store closures were likely. Industry analysts expressed concern that the losses could worsen if the downturn continues. Employees protested outside the headquarters following news of the job cuts.
